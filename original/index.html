How to play

  • Press Start, and the character begins running on its own.
  • Tap, click, or press Space or the Up arrow to jump. Press it again in mid-air for a double jump.
  • Jump over the crates, rocks, and cones on the ground. Touching one ends the run.
  • Pass through stars to collect them. Each star adds 25 points, and you also score points for distance.
  • The speed increases gradually during the run. The best score is kept for this session.
